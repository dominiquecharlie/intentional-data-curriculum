Foundational sources
The 9 sources the curriculum is built on
The segment map above shows where material is adapted, segment by segment. This list is the underlying evidence base, maintained by Intentional Data Research Watch. Last regenerated 2026-07-25.
Australia's 8 AI Ethics Principles
Its plain language makes it the scaffold participants use when sorting scenarios, without needing a legal background. Department of Industry, Science and Resources, Australian Government, 2019. www.industry.gov.au/publications/australias-artifici
CARE Model and Communities in the Loop
It supplies the community anchor exercise and the community impact category scored in the tool evaluation rubric. Measure, Measure, 2026. communityintheloop.org/
The AI Governance Stack: A Framework for the Next Three Years
It is the structure behind the governance charter and the maturity assessment leadership commits to. William Fedorich, AI Governance for Leaders, 2026. williamfedorich.substack.com/p/the-ai-governance-sta
Scenario-Based AI Literacy Scale (SAILS)
It is the basis of the literacy diagnostic and the reason upskilling can be measured before and after delivery instead of surveyed. Scheibenzuber et al., British Journal of Educational Technology, 2026. doi.org/10.1111/bjet.70065
Interrogative Reasoning and the Problem with the Human in the Loop
It is the basis for moving from human in the loop to communities in the loop, and it is credited to Measure, the curriculum's partner. Styles, Williams, Teran, and Johnson, Just Tech, Social Science Research Council, 2026. just-tech.ssrc.org/articles/interrogative-reasoning-
ISO/IEC 42001, AI management systems
It gives the governance architecture options a recognised external reference an auditor or funder will know. ISO and IEC, ISO, 2023. www.iso.org/standard/42001
AI Risk Management Framework
Texas TRAIGA names it as the safe harbor standard, which makes it the reference point for organizations operating in Texas. National Institute of Standards and Technology, NIST, 2023. www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
Policy on the AI Exponential, including the Advanced AI Framework
Its four risk categories are the taxonomy the governance charter uses, and it supplies the pace of change argument behind the why now framing. Anthropic, Anthropic, 2026. www.anthropic.com/policy-on-the-ai-exponential
What is California's AI safety law?
SB 53 is the pattern for state-level AI regulation, and its whistleblower provisions are modelled directly in the governance charter. Alikhani and Kane, Brookings Institution, 2025. www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-californias-ai-sa
